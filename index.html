Hire Freelancers in Pakistan

PakFreelance is Pakistan's freelance marketplace, connecting skilled Pakistani freelancers with clients at home and abroad. Whether you need a web developer in Lahore, a graphic designer in Karachi, a content writer in Islamabad, or a video editor working remotely — find vetted talent, agree milestones, and pay securely with escrow protection.

Popular freelance services in Pakistan

  • Web development & mobile apps
  • Graphic design & branding
  • Content writing & copywriting
  • Video editing & animation
  • Digital marketing & SEO
  • Virtual assistance & data entry
  • Urdu–English translation
  • Online tutoring & coaching

Why hire on PakFreelance?

Escrow payments with milestone releases, verified freelancer badges, skill-test quizzes, transparent 5% fee, and payouts via JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer in PKR. Post your job for free and get AI-matched proposals from Pakistan's top freelancers.
