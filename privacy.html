Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. The short version

This showcase of PakFreelance runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and no server that collects your personal information. Almost everything you do here stays on your own device.

2. What is stored, and where

  • On your device: your activity in the showcase — jobs you post, proposals you send, orders, messages, saved jobs, language and currency preferences — is stored in your browser's local storage (key: pakfreelance_v1) so the app remembers it between visits. It is never transmitted to us.
  • Payout details: if you add a sample payout method, the details are stored only in your browser and are always displayed masked (only the last four digits are shown).
  • Sample data: the freelancer profiles, photos, jobs, and reviews in this showcase are fictional. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.

3. Third-party content

Profile photos and portfolio images are loaded from public placeholder image services (pravatar.cc and picsum.photos). Those services may receive your IP address and browser information when images load, under their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

4. If you contact us

If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your email address or phone number and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply to you. We do not sell contact details or share them with third parties for marketing.

5. Your choices

You can erase everything the showcase has stored at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. To ask about information you have sent us by email or WhatsApp, or to ask us to delete it, write to aqilashah955@gmail.com.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
