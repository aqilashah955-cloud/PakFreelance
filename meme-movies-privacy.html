Privacy Policy — Meme Movies Pin Publisher

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. What this app is

Meme Movies Pin Publisher is a small tool used by the owner of the Meme Movies YouTube channel to publish his own video pins to his own Pinterest boards. It is not a public service and has no other users.

2. What information the app accesses

With the owner’s authorization through Pinterest’s official API, the app accesses only the owner’s own Pinterest account information, board list, and pins, so it can publish the pins the owner has chosen. It does not access, collect, or store information about any other Pinterest user.

3. What is stored, and where

  • Authorization tokens: the app stores the Pinterest OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to publish pins. They are kept securely and are never shown to anyone.
  • Publishing log: the app keeps a private log of which pins it published and when, so the owner can check what went out.
  • Nothing else: the app does not collect names, email addresses, locations, or any other personal information from anyone.

4. What is shared

The only information the app sends anywhere is the pin content the owner chose to publish — video, title, description, and link — sent to Pinterest through its official API so the pin appears on the owner’s boards. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with any third party for advertising or marketing.

5. Your choices

Because this app serves only its owner, there are no accounts to delete and no settings for anyone else to change. The owner can revoke the app’s Pinterest access at any time from his Pinterest account settings, which immediately stops it from publishing.
