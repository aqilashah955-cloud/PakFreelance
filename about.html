About PakFreelance

PakFreelance is a freelance marketplace built for Pakistan's growing pool of digital talent — web developers, graphic designers, content writers, video editors, digital marketers, SEO specialists, virtual assistants and translators — and for the clients who want to hire them, in Pakistan and around the world.

How it works

For clients: post a job for free, compare proposals from verified freelancers, and pay into escrow. Your money is released only when you approve the work. Clients can pay by card, Wise, or Payoneer.

For freelancers: build a profile, pass a skill test, and bid on jobs that match your expertise. When a job is done, withdraw your earnings to JazzCash, Easypaisa, or your bank account.

Why PakFreelance

  • Global payments in, local payouts out. Clients pay the way they prefer — card, Wise, or Payoneer — while freelancers receive money through the channels they actually use in Pakistan: JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank transfer.
  • Escrow protection on every order. Funds are held safely and released milestone by milestone, only on approval.
  • A transparent 5% fee. No hidden charges — the fee is shown before you confirm any order.
  • Verified, skill-tested freelancers. Profiles carry ID-verification and skill-test badges, so clients can hire with confidence.
  • Prices in PKR or USD. Switch currencies in the header; USD prices use an approximate rate (1 USD ≈ Rs 280).
