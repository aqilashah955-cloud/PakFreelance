1. The service

PakFreelance ("PakFreelance", "we", "us") is a freelance marketplace that connects clients with independent freelancers in Pakistan. Clients post jobs, receive proposals, fund orders through escrow, and release payment when work is approved. Freelancers build profiles, bid on jobs, and withdraw earnings to JazzCash, Easypaisa, or a bank account. Clients may pay by card, Wise, or Payoneer.

2. Sample data in this showcase

This public showcase of PakFreelance runs entirely on sample data. All freelancer profiles, jobs, reviews, proposals, messages, orders, and payout methods shown are fictional examples created to demonstrate how the marketplace works. No real contracts are formed, no real payments are processed, and no real funds move through this showcase. By using it, you acknowledge that everything you see is illustrative.

3. Escrow and fees

In the full product, client payments for an order are held in escrow and released to the freelancer only when the client approves the delivered work or a milestone. PakFreelance charges a flat 5% platform fee on each order, shown in full before an order is confirmed. Prices are displayed in PKR; where USD prices are shown, they use an approximate rate (1 USD ≈ Rs 280) and are for guidance only.

4. User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information in profiles, job posts, and proposals.
  • Do not post unlawful, infringing, fraudulent, or misleading content.
  • Do not share personal contact details in a way that circumvents the platform's safety features.
  • Freelancers are independent contractors, not employees of PakFreelance.

5. Disputes

If a client and freelancer cannot resolve an issue on an order, either party may open a dispute. Our team reviews the order history and communications and makes a final decision on the release of escrowed funds.
